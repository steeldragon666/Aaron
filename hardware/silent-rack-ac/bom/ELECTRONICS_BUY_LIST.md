# Electronics buy list - SRA-16

Everything electronic for the rack: the touchscreen, the rack node, sensors, cables, power, the office air kit, the PoE switch's power and a bench kit for the AVAS. Generated by `tools/make_buy_list.py` (CSV: `bom/electronics_buy_list.csv`); the refs match `drawings/CP-SRA16-ELC-001.pdf` and `docs/PRINTED_PARTS.md`.

**Rules used:** Temu wherever it is a sensible source. Anything that plugs into mains (the 12 V plug pack, the 48 V supply, the USB charger) must carry the **RCM** mark, so it comes from an Australian retailer or distributor. Parts that are widely faked and matter (Noctua fans, the Waveshare 4.3B screen) come from the maker or an authorised reseller unless the Temu listing clearly checks out. Prices are indicative AUD for the pack size usually sold; I could not check live listings, so confirm at checkout.

## Before you order

- **Touchscreen:** it must be the **4.3B**: the node talks to it over RS485 and the alarm buzzer hangs on its isolated output. The plain 4.3 has neither.
- **ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1:** the firmware and the carrier are drawn for this board (2 x 22 pins). The WROOM-32 breakout you have is a different chip and pinout; keep it as a bench spare.
- **DS18B20 probes:** cheap probes are sometimes clones. They work in this design (3-wire, powered), but buy a spare and check each one reads within 0.5 degC of the others in a glass of water.
- **Buck module:** the adjustable ones ship at a random voltage. Set 5.0-5.1 V with a meter before you connect the DevKit.
- **48 V supply:** read the switch's label first (DC input range, PoE budget). Most 16-port industrial PoE switches want 48-57 V; PoE+ cameras need about 52-54 V at the switch.
- **Mains items:** only RCM-marked plug packs and supplies inside a sealed rack.
