04 · Engineering principles

Intelligence you can put in charge — and still trust.

P-01

Whole-entity, non-linear

Value hides in the couplings between units. AIT optimises the entity as one non-convex system, not a stack of locally tuned islands — capturing the outcomes no single subsystem can reach alone.

P-02

Bounded autonomy

Intelligence proposes; certified safety disposes. Every action is held inside hard engineered envelopes, with deterministic protection that no probabilistic system can override.

P-03

Provable by design

Nothing acts unauthenticated and nothing is altered undetectably. Cryptographic assurance and an immutable record make every decision attributable, auditable and defensible.

P-04

Sovereign & portable

One architecture, many entities. AIT is built to be owned and operated under its operator's control, and to federate across sites, sectors and industries.
